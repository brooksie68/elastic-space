## 2026-09-04 — Claude (Fable 5.1) — Step 6: chapters 7–12, right-click ions, and copper

James's go ("copper real block… ions: let's go with your idea… be creative!"). Two new engine behaviours and six chapters:

RIGHT-CLICK IONIZE (`ionize(sc,a)`, wired on the bench's contextmenu; the right button never starts a drag or X-ray): an atom on its own loses an electron (givers, hydrogen, copper, carbon) or gains one (nitrogen through chlorine), the balance line tips, a warm or cool ring flashes out, a gliss plays, and a plain line says what happened ("Sodium lost an electron: 11 protons, 10 electrons, charge +1. It is an ion now, Na⁺."; hydrogen: "What is left is a bare proton, H⁺, and that is what an acid hands out."). Right-click again = neutral. Bonded atoms refuse ("pull it free first"); full shells refuse ("holds its electrons harder than anything"). Ions attract by charge and tether, and a tethered Na⁺/Cl⁻ pair is recognized as salt.

COPPER, THE FIRST METAL (`Cu` in ELEM, kind `metal`; KEYORD gained Cu; the copper tile unlocks): no hands, no lone pairs, no slots. Copper bonds only to copper, up to six neighbours each (`METAL_MAX`), a `metallic` bond with no lens: a faint bridge and two electrons drifting along it in opposite directions, never settling — the electron sea. A lone copper shows its one loose electron wandering the ring. Six pack into the hexagonal block on their own. Metallic bonds break under heat like singles (melting). `sc.onMetal(n)` reports the block size; a whisper on the first block of three.

THE CHAPTERS (guide.js, waits `ion:SYM` and `metal:N` added): 7 Ions on their own (Na⁺, Cl⁻, the tethered pair as salt, H⁺ = acid), 8 Carbon the builder (methane, ethane, propane — hydrogens first so each carbon keeps its places, then carbon to carbon), 9 Rings and double bonds (ethene by the two-hydrogens-each rule; benzene as a drawing and text — the min-free-hands bond rule makes a hand-built ring a puzzle, so it is not asked for), 10 Alcohol and sugar (methanol hands-on; glucose as a drawing), 11 Chains of life (ammonia hands-on; glycine's atoms handed with a drawing, no wait — "if you want to try it"; the peptide in text), 12 Metals (six coppers → the block, the sea, melting, and the closing line for all twelve). Every target has its drawing in the strip. The finish screen now says all twelve are done.

Verified in the pane at a 1920×1080 bench, engine stepped by hand: chapter 7 all four waits (Na⁺, Cl⁻, tether → salt, bare H⁺ with the small ring), chapter 8 methane, chapter 9 ethene lands CC2 by the rule, chapter 12 six coppers → 9 metallic bonds and the wake; console clean.
