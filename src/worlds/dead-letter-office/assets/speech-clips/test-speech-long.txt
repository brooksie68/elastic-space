Every letter here was written by someone who believed in an address. That's the whole faith of the thing - you fold up a piece of yourself, you name a place in the world where you think a person is standing, and you let go of it. Most of the time the world obliges. But down here we keep the ones where it didn't: the street that got renamed, the apartment that emptied out, the name spelled the way it sounded rather than the way it was. People assume this is a room full of failure. It isn't. It's a room full of intention that outlived its target - which, if you think about it, is most of what any of us leave behind. I don't open them to be nosy. I open them because a letter unread is a question still hanging in the air, and somebody ought to be the one who hears it, even if he's not the one it was meant for.
